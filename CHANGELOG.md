# Changelog

Every revision of the privacy policy, newest first. The extension version is
the release whose data handling the revision describes.

## 9 November 2026 (extension v0.11.0)

- Section 2 lists the price floors, price-move alert rules and launch dates saved by the Insights page (extension v0.11.0), and notes that Insights makes no network request.
- Section 5 describes the v0.10.0 behaviour: Daily updates older than the 7 or 10 day window are merged into one point per listing, not deleted.
- Clarified that background tabs opened by Daily Tracking carry the user's normal site cookies.
- Added section 9, "Legal basis and your rights" (DPDP Act 2023, GDPR).
- Added an email contact alongside GitHub issues.
- Added a table of contents, section anchors, a print stylesheet and this revision history.
- Repository: added `scripts/check-policy-sync.mjs`, a snapshot of the extension's `manifest.json` (v0.11.0) for that check, GitHub Actions checks, LICENSE and this file.

## 9 November 2026 (extension v0.9.1)

## 8 November 2026 (extension v0.8.7)
