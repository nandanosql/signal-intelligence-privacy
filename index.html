Signal Intelligence - Privacy Policy

Applies to the Signal Intelligence Chrome extension. Effective 9 November 2026.

In short
  • Everything the extension saves stays in your own browser. The developer runs no server and receives no data.
  • No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no selling or sharing of data.
  • Your YouTube API key, if you add one, is stored only on your device and is sent only to Google's YouTube Data API.
  • You can delete everything at any time.

1. What the extension does #

Signal Intelligence helps you follow the Indian phone market. It reads phone product pages on Flipkart, Amazon.in, mi.com and the vivo India E-Store that you choose to capture, keeps a local price history, compares models, shows public news and community posts about those models, and can fetch public YouTube comments for videos you ask about.

2. Data the extension stores #

All of the following is stored locally in the extension's storage in your browser (chrome.storage.local, and chrome.storage.session for the YouTube cache). None of it is transmitted to the developer.

The extension does not read or store your browsing history, your logins, cookies, account details, payment details, addresses, or the contents of pages other than the product pages you capture or track.

3. Network requests #

The extension makes requests directly from your browser, only to the sites below, and only after you grant access to each one. There is no developer server in between.

SiteWhen
www.flipkart.com, www.amazon.in, www.mi.com, shop.vivo.comWhen you turn on Daily Tracking, the extension opens each tracked product page in a background tab, reads the price details, and closes the tab
news.google.comWhen Social Feeds refreshes. The request carries search words such as the phone model names you track
www.reddit.com, www.smartprix.comWhen Social Feeds refreshes. These are plain feed downloads and carry no search words
Any https site you add as a custom feedOnly for feeds you add yourself
www.googleapis.com (YouTube Data API)Only when you fetch YouTube comments. The request contains your API key and the video address or search words you entered

When Daily Tracking opens a tracked product page in a background tab, your browser sends that site the same cookies it would send on a normal visit. If you are signed in to Flipkart, Amazon.in, mi.com or the vivo E-Store, that site will see the visit as yours, exactly as if you had opened the page yourself. The extension does not read, copy or store those cookies, and it adds nothing to the request beyond the page address.

These sites receive ordinary web requests from your browser and handle them under their own privacy policies. The extension uses YouTube API Services; by using that feature you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

5. How long data is kept #

  • Daily updates of tracked listings: the last 7 or 10 days, as you choose in Daily Tracking. Captures you save yourself, tracked or not, are kept until you delete them.
  • Social Feeds items: up to 500 items, and items older than 90 days are removed.
  • YouTube results: cached for the current browser session only.
  • YouTube API key: kept until you clear it.

6. Your controls #

  • Delete individual price observations and competitor sets, clear saved feed items, or clear the YouTube API key from inside the extension.
  • Turn Daily Tracking off at any time; its schedule is removed immediately.
  • Export YouTube comment results as CSV or JSON. Exports are files saved to your own device, and they never contain your API key.
  • Removing the extension from Chrome deletes all of its stored data.

7. Sharing and selling #

The developer does not collect, sell, rent, share or transfer your data to anyone, does not use it for advertising, creditworthiness or lending, and does not use it for any purpose unrelated to the features described above.

8. Children #

The extension is a market-research tool for adults and is not directed at children.

9. Legal basis and your rights #

The extension is built for people following the Indian phone market, so India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 is the law most likely to apply to you. Because the developer never receives, stores or has access to any of your data, the developer does not process your personal data and has nothing to share, correct or erase on your behalf. The same is true under the GDPR and similar laws elsewhere.

Any personal data that the extension handles, such as the display names of people who commented on a YouTube video, is processed only on your own device, under your control, and is covered by the controls in section 6. You are responsible for how you use data you export.

Access to the third-party sites listed in section 3 is governed by their own policies, and any rights you have against them are exercised with them directly.
